About Us

Who we are, what we believe, and how we work.

Who We Are

Dust Busst is a professional facility management and housekeeping service provider based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Our mission is to deliver dependable cleaning and facility support services through trained manpower, systematic working methods and attention to detail.

We believe cleanliness is not just about appearance; it is about health, comfort, safety and creating a better environment for everyone.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognized facility management service provider by delivering consistent quality and professional customer care.

Our Mission

  • Deliver reliable housekeeping services.
  • Maintain high standards of hygiene.
  • Build long-term customer relationships.
  • Provide professional and responsible manpower.
  • Continuously improve service quality.
